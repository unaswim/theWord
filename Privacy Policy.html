Privacy Policy The Word: Bible Quiz · Effective 26 October 2026 The short version • The Word does not collect, send or share any personal information. • The app has no internet access. It can't send anything anywhere. • No accounts, no ads, no analytics, no tracking. • Your progress stays on your phone. Uninstalling the app deletes it. Who we are The Word is a Bible quiz game for children and families, made by Unaswi Mothuti ("we", "us"). This policy explains how the app handles information. It applies to The Word on Google Play and Huawei AppGallery. Information we collect None. We do not collect personal information from anyone, including children. The app does not ask for a real name, email address, phone number, photo, location or contacts. It does not request permission to use the internet, camera, microphone, location or storage. What the app keeps on your device To remember your game, the app saves a few things in the phone's own private app storage: • your chosen language and sound setting • your explorer name (picked at random from a list, not your real name), avatar and age group • your game progress: XP, level, streaks, badges and which questions you have answered This information never leaves your device. We cannot see it, and nobody else receives it. You can erase it at any time with Me → Reset progress, or by uninstalling the app. Children The Word is designed for children as well as adults. Because the app collects no personal information, it collects none from children under 13 (or the age set by the law where you live). There are no chats, no contact with other players, no links out of the app, no ads and no in-app purchases. Sharing with others We do not sell, rent or share information with anyone, because we do not have any. The app contains no third-party advertising, analytics or tracking tools. App stores Google Play and Huawei AppGallery may collect information when you download the app, such as for installs and crash reports you choose to share. That is covered by their own privacy policies, not this one. Bible text Quotations in the app come from the King James Version (KJV) unless stated otherwise. Changes to this policy If a future version of the app adds features that use information, such as an online version or a parent account, we will update this policy before that version is released and change the date at the top.
